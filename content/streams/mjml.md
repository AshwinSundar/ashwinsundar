Emails are notoriously a pain to display nicely. inline styles are the way to go, and layouts are done via tables like it's 1999. I spent a couple hours trying to design templates in django before getting fed up with debugging tables, and searched for "Markdown for emails". I found MJML (Mailjet Markup Language), which is actually quite nice to use. I spent the first part of the week reading docs and integrating MJML into the dev environment. I feel more confident about sending templated emails now from MouseHouse!
