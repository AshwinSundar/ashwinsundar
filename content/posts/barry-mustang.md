Barry is desperate. Three rounds had passed him, and each time he failed to place the winning bid. Finally, after outbidding his fellow contestants by a single dollar, his chance has come.

Barry is now on stage. As he guesses more and more numbers, the brand new car comes tantalizingly into view. He now has one last chance - guess the correct number out of three choices, and the car is his!

Barry is in a daze as he shakily utters, "Four." Correct! The car is his! Barry exclaims, "I can't believe it! I told everyone that I was going to win a car, and I did it!"
