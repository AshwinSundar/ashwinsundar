Yesterday was my last day working for DEPT. I started at Devetry (now DEPT) in January 2022, hoping to round out my software education by working on a wide variety of projects for a lot of different industries. I got real-world experience with production software in Python, Go, and C++; built large-scale systems with Terraform, AWS, and MQTT; created complex features like authentication/authorization systems; and worked in a half a dozen industries, including medical device, satellite communications, and retail, and for the US government.

Now I'm trying out self-employment. MouseHouse is nearly feature complete for a version 1.0, so September will be focused on getting this feature - animal colony management - out to researchers who work with mice. I have one customer right now (whose name ends in "arvard"), which is enough proof for me that there's something here worth pursuing.

My school and career path to get here has been pretty bizarre. I tried out pre-med at UCLA, but wasn't the right fit. I worked in neuroscience research labs in undergrad and grad school, and along the way found out how much fun programming and computer science is. I thought I'd take a few classes to make me a better biomedical engineer. Turns out that programming was so much fun that I spent 10 years writing code! Now that I have the skillset I wanted, it's time to turn back to research and focus on making useful and cool tools for the field I came from.

Since I'm now my own boss, I've decided to start myself off as an intern at Sundar Software LLC! Let's see how week 1 goes and if a promotion is in store :)
